The #1 reason players keep coming back to a game they don't have fun playing anymore. 

## Defined

Sunken cost is a known human tendency to continue an undertaking because of the time, money, or effort invested in it (1). When applied to video games, designs can be tailored to exploit this tendency, such as grinding and playing by appointment.
