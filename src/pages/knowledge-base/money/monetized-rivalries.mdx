Want to be #1? Rank high in the leaderboards? Prepare to crack the piggy bank to compete with other players.

## Defined
Games that take advantage of player's competitive spirit, encouraging them to spend money to achieve status. Montizied rivalries leaderboards are often determined by the amount of money spent by players to gain a competitive edge (pay-to-win).
